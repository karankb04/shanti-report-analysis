# Website Performance Report

**Shanti Travel — www.shantitravel.com**
Period: 1 April – 31 August 2026 · Compared with 1 April – 31 August 2025
Source: Google Analytics 4, property 352360511 · Prepared 1 September 2026

> **A note on the numbers.** Traffic and engagement figures are reliable for both years. Lead figures are reliable only from **July 2026** onward, because conversion tracking was not installed on the website before then, and the current setup counts each lead twice. Lead counts in this report have been corrected for that. Guest forms on `connect.shantitravel.com` are excluded throughout — this report covers the marketing website only. Full detail in the accompanying data quality annex.

## 1. Summary

| Metric | Apr–Aug 2025 | Apr–Aug 2026 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sessions | 94,540 | 51,678 | **−45.3%** |
| Users | 71,772 | 42,414 | **−40.9%** |
| New users | 69,756 | 41,083 | −41.1% |
| Engagement rate | 72.2% | 65.4% | −6.8 pts |
| Avg. time on site | 2m 54s | 3m 03s | **+5.2%** |
| Pages per visit | 2.25 | 2.35 | **+4.3%** |
| Bounce rate | 27.8% | 34.6% | −6.8 pts |
| Inquiry starts (Jul–Aug) | 1,477 | 1,502 | **+1.7%** |
| Inquiry start rate (Jul–Aug) | 3.6% | **6.8%** | **+89%** |
| Leads (Jul–Aug) | not tracked | ~716 | — |

**The short version.** Visits are down 45% against last year. Inquiry volume is not. We received roughly the same number of inquiries from half the traffic, which means the rate at which visitors ask for a trip has almost doubled. Visitors also stay slightly longer and read slightly more pages than a year ago.

The traffic decline is concentrated in two channels and is partly a spending decision rather than a performance problem (section 3). The site is smaller but working harder.

**Two channels account for 87% of the decline.** Paid Search lost 20,731 sessions and Organic Search lost 15,912 — together 36,643 of the 42,862 total.

The Paid Search fall is the largest single number in this report and is most likely a budget decision rather than a performance issue. It should be confirmed against actual spend before being read as a problem. *(Spend figures were not available for this report — adding them would let us show cost per lead by channel, which is the missing half of this section.)*

**Paid Social is the one channel growing** — up 60% to 2,304 sessions, and in July–August it produced the highest inquiry rate of any paid channel.

**AI Assistant is new.** 174 sessions from ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini — 0.3% of traffic, but these visitors read 3.0 pages per visit against a site average of 2.35, the deepest of any source. Worth watching rather than acting on.

**Email is down 64%** with only two recorded conversions despite the best content engagement on the site (3.6 pages per visit). Something is breaking at the end of that journey.

## 6. Inquiries (July–August 2026)

Reliable data begins in July. In two months the site produced approximately **716 inquiries** from 22,087 sessions — an inquiry rate of **3.2%**.

The inquiry form is a seven-step process. Progress through it:

| Step | People | Still in |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1. Destinations | 1,414 | 100% |
| 2. Travellers | 1,322 | 93% |
| 3. Dates | 1,308 | 93% |
| 4. Budget | 1,280 | 91% |
| 5. Trip stage | 1,221 | 86% |
| 6. Their story | 1,193 | 84% |
| 7. Contact details | 1,062 | 75% |
| **Submitted** | **696** | **49%** |

The form works well for six steps — only 16% of people drop out across all of them. Then **a third of those who reach the final step do not submit**: 366 people in two months who chose a destination, gave dates and budget, wrote us about their trip, and stopped at the contact details.

That is roughly half again as many inquiries as we actually received, lost at the last click.

---

## 7. Recommendations

**1. Investigate the final step of the inquiry form.**
366 people in two months completed the entire form and did not submit. Recovering even a third of them would add around 120 inquiries per two months. Most likely causes are a required field people won't give (typically phone number) or a fault on mobile. Testable in an afternoon.

**2. Review the mobile experience on the top destination pages.**
Mobile is 71% of traffic, lost 11 points of engagement year-on-year while desktop held steady, and converts 24% worse. On `/fr/voyage-inde` and `/fr/voyage-bali` mobile converts four to five times worse than desktop. Likely the same underlying cause as recommendation 1.

**3. Correct the conversion tracking.**
Each lead is currently recorded twice, and guest forms are counted as sales leads. This makes every conversion figure unreliable and prevents any comparison with previous years. A one-off configuration fix; without it, next quarter's report has the same limitation as this one.

Worth noting alongside these: `/fr/departs-garantis-petit-groupe` has the site's best engagement and no way to enquire, and `/fr/trips` converts at twice the site average with very little traffic. Both are small changes.

## 8. Methodology and limitations

**Source.** Google Analytics 4 Data API, property 352360511, pulled 1 September 2026. Marketing website (`www.shantitravel.com`) only.

**Periods.** Traffic and engagement compare 1 Apr–31 Aug 2026 against the same months in 2025. Inquiry data covers 1 Jul–31 Aug 2026 only.

**Limitations:**

| Limitation | Effect |
|---|---|
| Conversion tracking installed July 2026 | No lead data before July 2026; no year-on-year lead comparison possible |
| Each lead recorded twice | Raw GA4 conversion counts are ~2× actual. Corrected throughout this report |
| Guest forms counted as leads | `connect.shantitravel.com` submissions excluded here; they represent 65% of the property's raw conversion count |
| Advertising spend not available | No cost per lead or return on ad spend in this report |
| Some untracked traffic | ~5% of sessions arrive without campaign tagging and cannot be attributed |

**Comparability.** The 2025 inquiry comparison uses form-start events, the closest equivalent measure available for that year. It indicates direction reliably but is not an exact like-for-like count.
